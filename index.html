Privacy Policy – shop_lucy_uk

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This application is used only by the eBay seller shop_lucy_uk (United Kingdom) to manage its own eBay listings, orders and buyer communication.

What data we process

  • eBay usernames of buyers who purchase from, message or leave feedback for shop_lucy_uk;
  • order details needed to fulfil a purchase (items, amounts, delivery city and postcode);
  • the content of messages and feedback exchanged on eBay.

Why

Only to fulfil orders, answer buyer questions and keep sales records. We do not sell or share this data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Where and how long

Data is stored securely on Cloudflare infrastructure. When eBay notifies us that a user has deleted their eBay account, that user's personal data is removed from our records.

Your rights

You can ask for access to, or deletion of, your data at any time: contact@nexuxco.uk.
